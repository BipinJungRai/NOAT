> [!tip]- ➕ Adding a case or reading
> Best place: the **📖 Cases & Readings** section of a module dashboard. Click **+ New** there and the note is filed here in `01 UNI/03 Literature` with `course` already set.
> - Set `type` in the table to `Case`, `Paper`, `Book Chapter`, or `Reading`.
> - Put any reading or case PDFs into `01 UNI/03 Literature/PDFs/` and link them in the `materials` column.
