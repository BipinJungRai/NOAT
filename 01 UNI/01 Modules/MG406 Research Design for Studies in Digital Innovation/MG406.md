# MG406 Research Design for Studies in Digital Innovation
*[[📚 Modules|⏪ All modules]] · [[🏠 Main Dashboard|🏠 Home]] · [[📊 Literature Dashboard|📖 Cases & Readings]] · [[📊 Concepts Dashboard|💡 Concepts]] · [[00 Start here|❓ How this works]]*

> [!tip]- ➕ Adding a note
> Click **+ New** (top-right of a table) in the section the note belongs to. The note is filed in the right folder with the module already filled in.
> - **Lecture / seminar / workshop / guest talk** → *🗓️ Classes*. Fill in `type`, `week` and `date` in the table.
> - **Case / paper / reading** → *📖 Cases & Readings* (goes into `03 Literature`). Fill in `type` (`Case`, `Paper`, `Book Chapter`, or `Reading`).
> - **Concept** → *💡 Concepts* (goes into `02 Concepts`)
> - **Assessment or other deadline** (exam, essay, sprint week, presentation slot) → *🎯 Deadlines* (goes into `Deadlines/`). Set `type` to `Assessment` if it's graded, otherwise `Deadline`.
> - Slides go in `00 Meta/`, reading & case PDFs go in `03 Literature/PDFs/`
