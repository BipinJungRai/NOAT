## What is a network effect?

A **network effect** is when each extra user makes a product more useful for everyone else. A phone is useless if nobody else has one, and very useful when everyone does. I wrote this up as a concept note: [[EXAMPLE - Network effect]].

> **Short answer:** value comes from how many people use it, not from how rare it is.

## What are the two kinds?

| Kind | What it means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct | More users of the same kind make it better | WhatsApp: more friends on it, more people to message |
| Indirect | More users on one side attract the other side | Uber: more riders attract more drivers, which means shorter waits |

## Why does this matter for managers?

Because the market can **tip**: once one product is clearly ahead, new users pick it because everyone else is there, and the gap keeps growing. That's why platforms often give the product away at first to reach enough users.
