> [!summary] In one line
> A product is more valuable to each user the more people use it.

## What is it?

When each new user makes the product more useful for everyone else. The value comes from the number of people connected, not from the product on its own.

> **Analogy:** a party is more fun the more of your friends are there. One person at a party is just a person in a room.

## Why does it matter?

It can make markets "tip" to a single winner, which is why platforms fight so hard for early users.
