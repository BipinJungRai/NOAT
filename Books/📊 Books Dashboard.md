# 📚 Books
*[[🏠 Main Dashboard]]*

> [!tip]- ➕ How to add a book
> - **A book you're reading:** click **+ New** in *📖 Reading now*. You get a note in `Books/` with `status: Reading` set. Fill in author, started, topic in the table.
> - **Any other book:** click the **+** at the top of a column on the *🗂️ Reading board* (To read, Finished…).
> - **Just the PDF:** drop it in `Books/` (or a `Books/<Title>/` folder). It appears in the *📄 PDF library*.
> - Link the PDF from the note's `pdf` property, and put an image link in `cover` to show a cover. `rating` is 1–5.
> - Tip: give each book a folder (`Books/<Title>/`) if it has images or a PDF.
