Do this once, using the example notes, and you'll understand the whole system.

## The tour

1. **Open a module.** On the **🏠 Main Dashboard**, click **MG472 · Socioeconomics**. This is a module dashboard: everything for MG472 in one place.
2. **Look at the example notes.** In the **🗓️ Classes** table you'll see two example notes. Click the lecture one to see what a finished note looks like. Come back with the ← arrow at the top-left.
3. **Rate a note by dragging.** Scroll down to the **🧠 Understanding board**. Drag the example seminar card from 🟠 Shaky to 🟡 Getting there. That's all rating is.
4. **Make your first note.** Back in **🗓️ Classes**, click **+ New** at the top-right of the table. A new note appears, already linked to MG472.

   ![The + New button on a module dashboard](images/new-button.png)

5. **Name it.** Click the note in the table to open it, click its title at the top (it says "Untitled"), and call it something like `MG472 W01 Lecture - Introduction`. Then come back with the ← arrow.
6. **Fill in the details.** In the table, click the empty cells for `type`, `week` and `date` and fill them in. For `type`, type `Lecture`.
7. **Write in it.** Open your note, press `Ctrl+E` / `Cmd+E` to start typing, and write a few lines. Try pasting a screenshot with `Ctrl+V` / `Cmd+V`.
8. **See the dashboard react.** Go back to the **🏠 Main Dashboard**. The **✅ To-do** section shows the tasks from the example notes.

> **That's the whole system:** make notes with **+ New**, rate them, and let the dashboards sort everything.

## Deleting the examples

When you're comfortable, delete them: find each one in the sidebar, right-click it, choose **Delete**, and confirm. They all start with `EXAMPLE`. An update will never bring them back.
