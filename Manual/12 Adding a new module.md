The compulsory Autumn Term modules are already set up. When you choose your Winter Term electives, add each one like this. It takes about 3 minutes per module.

## Step 1: Make the module's folder

1. In the left sidebar, open `01 UNI`, then right-click `01 Modules` and choose **New folder**.
2. Name it with the module code and title, e.g. `MG4J8 Managing Artificial Intelligence`.
3. Right-click your new folder and make two folders inside it: `00 Meta` (for slides) and `Deadlines`.

## Step 2: Copy an existing dashboard into it

1. Open any module folder, e.g. `MG472 Management and Socioeconomics of Digital Innovation`.
2. Right-click its dashboard (the note called `MG472`) and choose **Make a copy**.
3. Drag the copy into your new folder.
4. Rename the copy to **just the module code**, e.g. `MG4J8`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The dashboard's name must be exactly the module code. Every note for the module links to it by that name (`course: [[MG4J8]]`).

## Step 3: Change the details at the top

Open your new dashboard and press `Ctrl+E` / `Cmd+E` to edit it. Change:

- the **title line** (the line starting with `#`), e.g. `# MG4J8 Managing Artificial Intelligence`
- the **properties** at the top: `code`, `module`, `term` (`WT` for Winter Term), `convenor`, `assessment` and `credit`

Leave everything else as it is. The tables and boards work out which module they're in by themselves, so they're ready to use straight away.

## Step 4: Add it to your electives list

Open the note **My electives** (in `01 UNI`, or click **🆕 My electives** in the 🎓 Uni card on the Main Dashboard) and add a row for the module. Your list also appears in the Winter Term section of the 📚 Modules page.

## Step 5: Add its deadlines

Use **+ New** in the new dashboard's 🎯 Deadlines section. See [10 Deadlines](10%20Deadlines.md).

> [!NOTE]
> Your new module and its dashboard belong to you, so template updates never touch them. Only the dashboards that came with the template get updated.

## Using NOAT for a different course

NOAT was made for our MISDI class, so it comes with the compulsory MISDI Autumn Term modules already set up: MG406, MG472, MG481, MG487 and MG496. Nothing else about it is MISDI-only, so it works for any course. You just swap the MISDI modules for your own.

> **The good news:** when you rename a note or folder, Obsidian updates every link to it automatically. Rename a module's dashboard, and every note linked to it follows along, so the tables and boards keep working.

### 1. Turn a MISDI module into one of yours

1. In the sidebar, right-click the module's folder (e.g. `MG472 Management and Socioeconomics of Digital Innovation`), choose **Rename**, and type your module's code and title.
2. Inside it, right-click the dashboard (e.g. `MG472`), choose **Rename**, and type just your module's code, e.g. `EC101`.
3. Open the dashboard, press `Ctrl+E` / `Cmd+E`, and change the title line and the properties at the top (`code`, `module`, `term`, `convenor`, `assessment`, `credit`).

Do this for each module you need. To add more modules than NOAT comes with, use the steps at the top of this page.

### 2. Delete the modules you don't need

Right-click the module's folder and choose **Delete**. This also deletes its deadline notes.

### 3. Delete the MISDI deadlines

The notes in each module's `Deadlines` folder are MISDI's exams and essays. Delete them, and add your own (see [10 Deadlines](10%20Deadlines.md)).

### 4. Update the module lists

Two pages list the modules by hand:

- the **🎓 Uni** card on the 🏠 Main Dashboard
- the term tables on the 📚 Modules page

Renamed modules still link correctly, but the words next to them (e.g. "MG472 · Socioeconomics") don't change by themselves, and links to deleted modules turn faded. Open each page, press `Ctrl+E` / `Cmd+E`, and edit the lists to match your modules.

### 5. Change the term dates (optional)

The line at the top of the Main Dashboard and the 📚 Modules page shows the current term and week (e.g. "AT · Week 4") using LSE's 2026–27 term dates. To use your own, open the page in Editing view, find the dates in that first line (e.g. `2026-09-28` for the start of term), and replace them with your own term's start and end dates. Or just delete the line.

> [!WARNING]
> The Main Dashboard and 📚 Modules page are 🔒 template files, so a NOAT update would replace your changes there. If you've adapted NOAT for your own course, don't copy those two files when you update, or redo your changes afterwards. See [15 Updating and what not to edit](15%20Updating%20and%20what%20not%20to%20edit.md).
