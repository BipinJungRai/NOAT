# 💼 Job Hunt
*[[🏠 Main Dashboard]] · [[00 Start here|❓ How this works]]*

> [!tip]- ➕ How to add a job
> 1. In **🔥 To apply** below, click **+ New** (top-right of the table). The note is filed in `Job Hunt/` with the `#job-application` tag and `status: Not applied` already set.
> 2. Name it `Company - Role`, then fill the columns straight in the table: company, deadline, link…
> 3. Paste the job description into the note. Suggested headings: *At a glance · When do I apply? · Am I eligible? · What would I do? · Why me? · How do I prepare? · Application log*.
>
> **Moving it along:** drag the card across the **🗂️ Board**, or change `status`:
> `Not applied` → `Applied` → `Online test` → `Interview` → `Assessment centre` → `Offer` / `Rejected` / `Withdrawn` / `Closed`
> When you apply, fill `applied_date`. When you hear back, fill `next_step` and `next_step_date` (e.g. "HireVue video" · 2026-10-30).
> Tasks with a 📅 date (e.g. `- [ ] Submit 📅 2026-10-20`) appear under *Job hunt tasks*.

> [!abstract]- 📋 Values to copy-paste (must match exactly, including capitals and emoji)
> | Property | Choose one of |
> |---|---|
> | `status` | `Not applied` · `Applied` · `Online test` · `Interview` · `Assessment centre` · `Offer` · `Rejected` · `Withdrawn` · `Closed` |
> | `sponsorship` | `Yes` · `No` · `Unknown` |
> | `programme` | `Full Time Graduate` · `Summer Internship` · `Spring Week / Insight` · `Placement` · `Experienced Hire` |
> | `career_path` | `Consulting` · `Tech / Product` · `Business Analysis` · `Strategy` · `Data / AI` · `Finance` |
> | `rolling` | tick the checkbox if there's no fixed deadline |
> | `deadline / applied_date / next_step_date` | Dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`, e.g. `2026-10-30` |
